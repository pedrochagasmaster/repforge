# Product concept: Taurifer

**A strength-training app that tells you what to lift next.**

Taurifer is a phone app for people who lift weights from a structured program. You log each set (load, reps, and how many reps you had left), and the app works out the weight and reps to aim for next time, with the reason written out.

## What it does

- **Builds a program for you.** Answer a few questions (goal, days per week, equipment, session length, muscles to prioritize) and get a multi-week program you can edit. Or pick a ready-made program, or paste/import the program you already follow.
- **Tells you the next target.** For every exercise: "Go up to 105 kg, aim for 6 reps" or "Hold 40 kg, aim for 8 reps", plus a "Why this weight" explanation based on what you did last time.
- **Fast logging in the gym.** One tap logs a set. Rest timer with notifications. Swap an exercise when a machine is taken. Works fully offline.
- **Shows progress.** Per-lift strength charts, records, weekly volume per muscle, session summary, an end-of-block review, and a shareable result card.
- **Exercise library.** 270 movements with names in English and Portuguese. 96 have an illustration; the rest have none. There are no exercise videos.
- **Share a program by link.** A coach or friend can send their program as a link; the receiver starts it in one tap. The sender never sees the receiver's training data.

## How it is delivered

- It is a **web app**. You open it in the phone browser and can add it to the home screen. It is **not in the App Store or Google Play**.
- **No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync.** Your training data stays on your phone only. To move to a new phone or keep a backup you export a file yourself (JSON or CSV).
- Languages: English and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Covers muscle building and general strength. It does not do powerlifting meet prep, cardio, nutrition, or wearable integration.
- It deliberately has **no streaks, badges, points, levels, social feed, or motivational messages**.

## Price

- **Free** today: building a program, logging, next-target recommendations, progress and export are all free.
- A paid **Pro** tier is planned at **R$24.90 per month or R$179.90 per year** (roughly US$4.50 / US$32). Pro would add more personalized program building, building your next program from your training history, and adjustments during a program when you keep skipping, stalling or running out of time. Everything free today would stay free.

## Screens

Three image sheets accompany this concept, four phone screens each:

1. First visit and program building: landing page, the three ways to start, a setup question, the recommended program.
2. In the gym: today's session, logging a set with the next target, the "Why this weight" explanation, the rest timer.
3. After training: session summary with share card, progress overview, strength charts, the program page.
