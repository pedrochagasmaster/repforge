# Surface brief: Round 4 · J · Conversa (onboarding tournament candidate)

Scope: the Round 4 core onboarding journey rendered by `round-4/candidates/j.js` + `j.css` (landing, route offer in the thread, Recommend questions, program message and corrections, activation, replacement and conflict, paste door with gaps and import review, Today hand-off). Mode: Operate (the lifter completes setup), with a Persuade opening on the landing.

Audience and job: a self-directed Brazilian lifter, PT-BR first, one-handed, who replies to a handful of fixed questions or pastes what they already train, checks the program and starts it. Every program comes from the real engine (TF/TS) and the shared fixtures. Constraints: 320–430 px, 200% text, light and dark, reduced motion, 44 px targets, no invented numbers, no celebration, no bull/forge/Latin, never a person and never an AI: Taurifer asks deterministic questions in its own plain voice.

Product decision reopened: PD-1 (no chooser). The routes are offered inside the thread: reply to the questions (Recommend), paste a program into the composer (paste door), or attach a Taurifer file (paperclip). Declared in the header comment, `policy.productDecisions` and `j.notes.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Setup is a chat you already know how to use. Taurifer asks, you answer by tapping a quick reply, your program arrives as a message, and you correct it by replying to the line you want changed. It refuses the category default of a card chooser followed by a numbered form wizard, and it refuses the chatbot costume: no typing indicator, no first person, no free-text understanding.

OWN-WORLD: Messaging-app grammar in Taurifer's own material. Concrete-sage wallpaper printed with faint training notation (3×8, RIR 2, +2,5 kg, ticks), a graphite app bar with the mark on a paper plate, white paper incoming bubbles with tails, signal-orange outgoing bubbles with ink text, pale amber system notices, quick-reply pills outlined in deep orange, single and double ticks, quoted-reply bars, "Colada" labels on pasted text, a pill composer with paperclip and round send button. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for pasted text and the prompt. Dark is the same chat at night: near-black wallpaper, charcoal bubbles, the orange voice unchanged.

STORY: The lifter reads one plain opening, sees the real Today it leads to, taps Montar meu programa, and answers eight short quick replies whose sent answers stay in the thread as their own orange messages. The program lands as a message they read top-down; they tap any line it was built from, reply with a new answer, and a new version arrives beneath with the count of exercises that changed. Usar este programa hands the chat over to Today.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing at 390×844: graphite app bar (mark plate, "Taurifer", status "Sem conta · neste dispositivo", Privacidade at right) across the top; an amber lock notice centred under it; a white image bubble holding the real Today capture cropped like a chat thumbnail; the opening message (headline in 800 at 26 px, body below) as the last white bubble; directly under it two full-width reply buttons attached to that bubble, Montar meu programa and Acompanhar meu programa atual, ending about 60 px above the bottom edge. The thread is bottom-anchored like an open chat.

FORM: Messaging interface grammar (WhatsApp-era quick replies, quoted replies, pasted and forwarded labels, ticks). Position 1 on the ordered list for this direction (the card J · Conversa, assigned by the Round 4 brief). Seed key 41ef581f. Signature interaction: Responder para corrigir. Tap or swipe right on any of your answers, or on any line of the program message; the composer rises as a reply sheet quoting that line, you pick the new answer and send; your reply bubble quotes the old line, a new version of the program arrives beneath it stating how many exercises changed, and the previous version folds into a one-line message. Supporting motion: a tapped quick reply lifts into the outgoing bubble position and gains its tail.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
