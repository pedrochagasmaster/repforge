# Surface brief: Round 4 candidate I · Ficha (onboarding, first run to Today)

Scope: the onboarding core journey of the tournament harness (landing, route choice, Recommend, the paste door, import review, review, activation, Today hand-off, replacement and conflict). Mode: Operate, with a Persuade landing. Audience: the self-directed Brazilian lifter, phone in hand at the gym desk. Constraints: harness contract (`round-2/JOURNEYS.md`, Round 4 brief), real engine through `TF`, PT-BR first, light and dark, 320–430 px, 200% text, reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Setup is filling in your own academy ficha de treino: every answer is written onto the card in your pen, the review is the completed card, activation date-stamps it and hands it to Today. Refuses the category default of a wizard of floating option cards over a neutral app ground.

OWN-WORLD: Coloured sulfite cardstock fills the screen (canary yellow for Recommend, sky blue for the paste door, pink, mint and white for the other routes) with a faint fibre grain; one-colour warm-black offset print for every printed word, rule and reversed header band; the lifter's BIC-blue ballpoint (Kalam) for every answer; one red rubber-stamp ink for activation and destructive marks. Parenthesis boxes "( )" for single choice, square boxes for multiple, hand-drawn X marks and circled numerals, perforated tear-off stubs for the pinned action and for dialog slips, a kraft card box for the route choice. Dark rendition: the carbon sheet (navy-black) where print and pen show as pale impressions.

STORY: The lifter sees their blank card, understands each question fills a named field on it, watches their answers accumulate in their own hand, reads the printed program on the finished card, corrects an answer by striking it through, and stamps it active.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canary card. Top: one-colour printed mark and wordmark, Privacidade at right. Printed display headline across the card (Sofia Sans Condensed 900, about 2.6rem). Below, the blank card itself: printed header fields with empty dotted lines and an empty EXERCÍCIO · SÉRIES · REPS · CARGA grid with a reversed black header band. Two actions under it, the primary as a reversed print block, both inside 844 px.

FORM: The academy ficha de treino, position 1 on the ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key 41ef581f. Signature interaction: "Escrito na ficha", each answer is handwritten into its printed field as it is chosen (pen-stroke reveal, drawn X and circles), fields stay tappable to correct with a strike-through, and the arc closes with the date stamp at activation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
