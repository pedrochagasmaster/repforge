# Surface: Round 4 onboarding candidate L · Pino

Scope: the core onboarding journey (landing, chooser, Recommend, paste/file import, review, activation, replacement/conflict, Today hand-off) in the tournament harness. Mode: Operate. Audience: a Brazilian lifter on a phone in a commercial gym, PT-BR first. Job: get a program the lifter owns and use it. Constraint: every program comes from the real engine; activation only when every answer is the lifter's own. Product decision reopened: PD-4 (a program from labelled defaults before the answers), declared in header, policy and notes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The program exists before the questions. Recommend opens on a real, compiled program built from labelled defaults ("ainda não é seu"); every answer is a selector pin the lifter moves while the program recompiles under their thumb. It refuses the questionnaire-then-result arrangement, and activation stays locked until all seven pins were moved by the lifter.

OWN-WORLD: The selectorized weight stack. Black powder-coat plates with small machined radii and a steel selector rod showing in the gaps, stencilled numerals and names (Big Shoulders Stencil Display), placard labels in Barlow Condensed, body in Barlow, a safety-yellow selector pin as the only chosen-state mark, yellow/black hazard stripe for "not yours yet", zinc-primer wall ground in light and powder-coat ground in dark. Plates, pins, placards and tags; no cards, no chips, no pills.

STORY: The lifter sees a usable-looking program at once, reads that it is not theirs, moves each pin and watches the exact number of changed exercises, and the machine locks into their program when the last pin goes in; bringing their own program is the machine's numbered instruction placard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390 px on Recommend: header row (Voltar, Cancelar); a full-width black readout plate with the program name in ~44 px stencil caps, the hazard tag "Ainda não é seu · 7 respostas no padrão", a facts line and Day 1's exercises; below it the first stack (Objetivo) with three plates and a hollow ghost pin in plate one; pinned at the bottom the locked "Usar este programa" plate with its reason.

FORM: L · Pino (machine weight stack), position 5 of 5 on the Round 4 brief's list, seed key 41ef581f. Signature interaction: the pin drop (the pin slides to the tapped plate, the readout recompiles and counts changed exercises; the last pin drops the hazard tag and locks the machine into the review).
