# Surface brief: taurifer.com landing, round 4 (candidates N and P)

Mode: Persuade. Mobile only. Visitor: a Brazilian lifter who has never opened Taurifer, reading on a phone. Action: Montar meu treino (persistent). Proof: real engine output (progression-engine.js via engine-cases.mjs) and real app renders only. Constraints: orange accent, IBM Plex, brand-guide voice, PT default with EN parity, round 3's message.

## Direction contract

THESIS (N, O próximo número): the page is the anatomy of one number, the weight for next time. Refuses the category's feature-grid landing and round 2's section stack. (P, Perguntas): the page is the lifter's own questions in the order they arrive, each answered by the engine. Refuses the pitch-then-features arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: N stays in the ledger world: warm paper, ink, hairlines, Plex Mono figures, orange only on what changed; scale is the drama. P commits the orange: every question owns a full-bleed orange field in white (light) or charcoal (dark) Plex Sans 650; every answer is paper that slides over it.

STORY: N: see 62,5 kg, watch it assemble from last session (sets, meta, RIR, rule), flip the outcome, see the app, start. P: ask "quanto coloco hoje", get the number, then log, RIR, failure, middle, spreadsheet, gym use, starting, data, price.

FIRST VIEWPORT: N: H1 over a viewport-wide 62,5 that rolls up from 60, one caption line naming the session; dock CTA. P: orange field with the H1 as the question, the answer 62,5 kg on paper beneath with one sentence; dock CTA.

FORM: surface structures 4 (N, lead) and 3 (P) of seven; seed key cba9b963 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
