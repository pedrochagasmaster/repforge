# Surface brief: Round 4 candidate H · Concreto (onboarding tournament)

Scope: first-run onboarding core journey (landing, route choice, Recommend walk, review, activation, paste door, import review), harness candidate `h`. Mode: Persuade on the landing and chooser, Operate from the first question on. Audience: a self-directed Brazilian lifter, phone in hand, PT-BR first. Constraints: real engine only (TF/TS), brand of a quiet training partner, 44 px targets, 320–430 px, light and dark, 200% text, reduced motion, no product decision reopened.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Onboarding is a sequence of concrete poems. Each screen is one typographic composition on a strict six-column grid: one monumental lowercase word states the decision, the answers are words placed in the composition, and the lifter's choice floods its cell with the single saturated field. It refuses the category arrangement of a stacked form: card rows with radio marks, a progress bar, a sticky summary card.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white page, true black ink, one saturated vermilion field (and its dark rendition on black). Jost (the open Futura lineage of the Noigandres group) is the only face: lowercase monumental words, tabular numerals, sentence-case prose. Square corners, 3–4 px black rules as structure, no cards, no shadows, no radius. Numeric scales are set as an ascending staircase across the grid. Buttons are words on a black or vermilion bar with a drawn arrow; choices are words in grid cells; dialogs are full-bleed black slabs with white words.

STORY: The lifter sees the promise as a poem, chooses how to start among five verbs, answers one decision per screen and watches their own answers assemble into a verse, then meets their program as a poster (days as columns of words), corrects any word of the verse in place with a true change count, and uses it. They believe Taurifer asked only what it needed and built exactly this.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390×844. Top: the Taurifer mark and name at left, Privacidade at right, on a hairline. Then the landing headline set as a concrete poem across the full width: "chegue na academia / sabendo" in black at ~34 px, then "exatamente" monumental (~64 px) reversed out of a full-bleed vermilion band, then "o que fazer." Below, the body line, then the two actions stacked full width: Montar meu programa (vermilion bar, drawn arrow) and Acompanhar meu programa atual (black rule outline). The real Today capture sits in the grid below the actions, framed by a black rule, top edge visible in the first viewport.

Signature interaction: "O verso" (the verse assembles). Every answer is set, as its short word, into a running verse at the head of the next composition; the chosen cell floods with the vermilion field in a left-to-right clip wipe. On the review the verse becomes the poster's colophon, and each of its words is the control that reopens that answer and recompiles in place.

FORM: A Noigandres-style concrete poetry broadside sequence (a poster series, not a form); position 1 on the roll's ordered list (the assigned lead); seed key 41ef581f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
